Why Crochet?

My crochet love all started with my aunt. My aunt always made little garments for my brother and I for Christmas. When I saw how amazing they were, I wanted to learn too. Since my aunt lives on the East Coast, it was hard for me to contact her, so I did research, bought kits, and learned from books. Now, I'm confidently crocheting projects for my friends and family.
