About My Project

In Tessellations' Mastery Elective, you get to choose something you'd really like to master (something you're passionate about) and explore it in depth. Because of how much I love crocheting (see "Why Crochet?"), I decided to focus on improving my skills and creating unique pieces.


My project was to design a cardigan for my personal usage, but along the way I got interested in so much more, like gloves, plushies, and colorwork. Right now, my project is generally just exploration with no clear destination, which is one of my favorite things to do: moving forward with no clear destination. I hope I can create some cool deliverables and have a lot of fun along the way!
